Hey, I'm Roey 👋

From Tel Aviv. In my free time I love building things from scratch. It always starts with a crazy idea — then I realize it's too dangerous, impossible, or just way too big — so I sketch on a small piece of paper what I actually can pull off.

Ideas come from design magazines, friends, and the internet. I hoard the cool ones and try to make them by hand. Most people would just buy the thing — but the satisfaction of building it yourself is unbeatable. That's why I'm here.
