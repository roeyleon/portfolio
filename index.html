Hey, I'm Roey 👋

From Tel Aviv, Israel. In my free time I love building stuff from scratch. It always starts with a crazy idea — and then I realize it's too dangerous, unachievable, or just too big — so I draw on a small piece of paper what I CAN do.

Ideas come from design magazines, friends, and the internet. I collect a bunch of cool ones and then try to create them with my bare hands. Most people would just buy the product — but the satisfaction of making something yourself is awesome. That's why I'm here.
